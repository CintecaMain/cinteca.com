cinteca.online

The sign-in and account service of the Cinteca group's applications.

Who operates it

The domain cinteca.online and the services under it are operated by AD Caldas Innotec SA, a company registered in Spain (CIF A66316399).

What it is for

cinteca.online hosts the sign-in and account service (OAuth) of the Cinteca group's applications: it lets people create an account, sign in and recover access to those applications.

It sends only transactional messages, such as account verification and password reset, and only to people who asked for them. It sends no advertising, newsletters or other bulk messages, and we do not authorise anyone else to send them in its name.

Report abuse or reach the postmaster

If you received a message from cinteca.online that you did not ask for, or want to report any other abuse, write to admin@cinteca.cloud. The postmaster of cinteca.online is reached at the same address. We investigate every report.

If you can, include the message with its full headers.

Privacy

  • This page sets no cookies, runs no scripts, uses no analytics or trackers and loads nothing from other sites.
  • Like any web server, ours records technical access data (IP address, date and time, page requested and browser identification) to operate and protect the service.
  • The account service uses the contact details you register to run your account, to sign you in to the group's applications and to send you the messages described above.
  • For questions about your personal data, write to admin@cinteca.cloud.
