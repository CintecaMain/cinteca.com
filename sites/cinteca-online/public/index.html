cinteca.online

The sign-in and account service of Cinteca's applications. Cinteca is the technology brand of AD Caldas Innotec, S.A.

Who operates it

The domain cinteca.online and the services under it are operated by AD Caldas Innotec, S.A., a company registered in Spain (NIF A66316399).

What it is for

cinteca.online hosts the sign-in and account service (OAuth) of Cinteca's applications: it lets people create an account, sign in and recover access to those applications.

It sends only transactional messages, such as account verification and password reset, and only to people who asked for them. It sends no advertising, newsletters or other bulk messages, and we do not authorise anyone else to send them in its name.

Report abuse or reach the postmaster

If you received a message from cinteca.online that you did not ask for, or want to report any other abuse, write to admin@cinteca.cloud. The postmaster of cinteca.online is reached at the same address. We investigate every report.

If you can, include the message with its full headers.

Privacy

  • This page sets no cookies, runs no scripts, uses no analytics or trackers and loads nothing from other sites.
  • Like any web server, ours records each request to operate and protect the service: the visitor's IP address (and that of the platform's proxy that passes the request on), the date and time, the request (method, page and protocol), the response status and size, the referring page and the browser identification. These records are kept for 30 days, or less if they exceed 1 GB; each server running the site also keeps the latest ones (up to 100 MB) until the site is next updated.
  • That proxy, which receives each visit before the web server, also records the IP address, the date and time, the address requested, the response status and size and the time taken; those records are kept for one year, or less if they exceed 1 GB.
  • The account service uses the contact details you register to run your account, to sign you in to Cinteca's applications and to send you the messages described above.
  • For questions about your personal data, write to admin@cinteca.cloud.
